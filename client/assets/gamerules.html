How to Play the Game

Brain Bank Heist is an educational game where you play as a thief trying to crack a vault. Answer multiple-choice questions across three levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Game Levels:
Layer 1: Easy Questions
Layer 2: Medium Questions
Layer 3: Hard Questions

Two-Strike Rule:
1st wrong answer: Retry
2nd wrong answer: Game Over

Winning:
Answer all questions correctly to crack the vault.

After Game:
View score, play again, or return home.
